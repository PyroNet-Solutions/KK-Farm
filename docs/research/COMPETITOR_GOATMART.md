## Context from comparable Pakistani platforms (for orientation only)

Search results surfaced several Pakistani online livestock platforms that appear to be **marketplaces oriented around Eid‑ul‑Azha / Qurbani sales** rather than farm-management tools: e.g. listing animals with species, breed, weight and price; home delivery; sacrifice-and-distribute services; and in one case a provincial (PITB-backed) cattle-market buyer app. This matches the brief's caution: marketplace features (listings, pricing, buyer search) are a different product from internal herd management.

Takeaways relevant to KK Farm (to validate, not adopt):
- Buyers in this market value **weight, breed, age and photos** — the same core attributes KK Farm must record accurately anyway. A future "sale listing export" could reuse them without building a marketplace.
- Urdu/English bilingual UI and WhatsApp contact are common patterns in this segment; relevant to Q-NFR-2 and Q-RB-3.
- None of the platforms found appear to offer pedigree, veterinary or feeding management — consistent with KK Farm's internal focus being the differentiator.
