# Farm Workflow Discovery — Interview and Observation Checklists

**Purpose:** capture how work actually happens at KK Farm — who performs each workflow, when, what is recorded, who approves, and what goes wrong — so the system models real routines rather than assumed ones.
**How to use:** one sheet per role; 20–40 minutes each; write answers in the person's own words; tag **R** (reported) unless you **O** (observed) or **C** (confirmed by a document). Use codes (`EMP-01`, `VET-1`), never names.
**Opening line for every interview:** "We are building a phone app to make your work easier and to keep goat records safe. Nothing you say will be used against anyone. Please tell us how things really happen, including problems."
