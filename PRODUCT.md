# KK Farm — Product context

Inferred from the owner's written brief; the owner asked to proceed without an interview. Items marked *(assumed)* are not confirmed.

## What it is
PyroNet Solutions' own livestock management platform, built from scratch, designed around KK Farm first and able to grow to more farms and SaaS later.

## Mechanism
Every goat has one permanent digital identity that outlives its RFID tags. Scan a tag and the whole animal opens: photographs, pedigree, health, weight, feeding, cost and full lifecycle history.

## Principle
Quality of goats, not quantity of goats. Individual animals and bloodlines are judged on evidence.

## People and scene
- CEO / owner: checks the farm from a laptop or phone, wants the state of the herd and the money at a glance.
- Veterinarians: work in the shed, on a phone, one goat at a time, accountable for clinical records.
- Caretakers, feeding and cleaning staff: limited technical education, phones in daylight, dirty hands, often shared devices; need scan-first, few-word, large-target flows.
- Finance: desk work, restricted access.

The farm is in Pakistan *(assumed from context: PKR, Urdu later)*. About 118 goats, one shed now, about six planned, multiple breeds.

## Register
Product. Operate mode. Premium, enterprise-grade, commercial SaaS quality; must never look like a basic admin template.

## Priorities (owner's order)
Reliability and data integrity, exceptional speed, real-time visibility, excellent mobile experience, premium visual design, security and access control, easy maintenance.

## Anti-references
Generic admin templates; marketplace look; copying competitors (Throf, Farmbrite, PeakHerds, UBIQ) in any way; decorative animation.

## Constraints
Synthetic demonstration data only, clearly labelled. Simulated functions labelled. Respect reduced motion. Smooth on mid-range Android. Never invent farm business rules.
