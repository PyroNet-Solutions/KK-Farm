## Direction contract

THESIS: The herd is a wall of individual tiles. Every goat is seen as one animal with its own identity, never as a row in a count. Refuses the category default of KPI cards over a grey table.

OWN-WORLD: Multani kashi glaze. Deep cobalt shell, turquoise action colour, cool glazed-white ground, manganese violet as third data colour. Square-shouldered 6px tiles, thin glaze edge, no gradients on data. One workhorse sans, tabular figures. Standard web controls throughout.

STORY: The owner sees herd state first, opens any tile, reads the goat's whole life, and acts. Staff scan, confirm, done.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt left rail with grouped navigation; top bar with search, simulated role switcher and synthetic-data label. Main area leads with the Herd Wall (all goats as tiles grouped by pen, glaze colour = status) with a legend that filters it; beside it, a needs-attention list and live activity; below, weight, finance and breeding charts.

FORM: Multani kashi glazed tile, position 7 on the ordered list. seed:c6116abfcbd91178

Signature interaction: Herd Wall tiles hold position; hovering shows the goat, clicking opens Goat 360; filters dim, never reorder. Each goat's portrait is generated from its identity, so no two tiles match.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
